## Alethe 1.7.5

A fix for updating from inside the app on Windows.

### 🔧 The update finishes and Alethe reopens

- **The installer is no longer killed when the app closes.** Alethe keeps its terminals and agents
  from outliving it, and that safeguard also took down the installer the update had just started.
  The version never changed and nothing reopened. The app now lets go of the safeguard right before
  handing over to the installer, and puts it back if the installer cannot start.

### ⚠️ One manual step if you are on 1.7.4 or earlier

- Those versions still have the problem, so they cannot update themselves to this one. Install 1.7.5
  once from the installer:
  [`Alethe-Setup.exe`](https://github.com/Vnxcarvalho/Alethe-2.0/releases/latest/download/Alethe-Setup.exe).
  Your projects, layouts and sessions are kept. From 1.7.5 on, the in-app update works.

This release ships for Windows only. The complete list of changes is in
[`docs/CHANGELOG.md`](https://github.com/Vnxcarvalho/Alethe-2.0/blob/main/docs/CHANGELOG.md).
